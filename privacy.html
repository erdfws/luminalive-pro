LuminaLive Pro 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日

LuminaLive Pro（以下简称“本 App”）是一款在 iPhone 本地运行的相机与照片、视频调色工具。我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

我们不收集的数据

本 App 没有账号系统，不包含任何广告、统计分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不会把你的照片、视频、编辑记录或设备信息发送到任何服务器。

照片与视频

只有在你授权后，本 App 才会读取相册，用于显示、编辑你选中的照片和视频；保存时，成品写回你的相册。所有处理都在你的设备上完成。你可以随时在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片”中更改或撤销授权。

相机与麦克风

只有在你授权后，本 App 才会使用相机拍照；拍摄实况照片时会使用麦克风录下拍照前后约 1.5 秒的声音。取景画面的风格处理、人脸和宠物识别、场景识别都在设备上实时完成，不会保存或上传画面。拍好的照片和实况视频只保存到你的相册。你可以随时在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 相机 / 麦克风”中更改或撤销授权。

保存在设备上的数据

为了让你稍后继续编辑，本 App 会把尚未保存的调整参数（不含照片本身）存放在本 App 的私有存储空间中，另外保存你选择的界面语言。这些数据只留在你的设备上；删除本 App 即会一并删除。

儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如果今后的版本改变了数据处理方式，我们会先更新本页面，并在 App Store 的隐私说明中如实标注。

联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 技术支持页面 联系我们。

LuminaLive Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

LuminaLive Pro ("the app") is a camera and photo & video grading tool that runs entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

Data we don't collect

The app has no accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs. It never sends your photos, videos, edits or device information to any server.

Photos and videos

Only with your permission, the app reads your photo library to show and edit the items you choose, and saves results back to your library. All processing happens on your device. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Camera and microphone

Only with your permission, the app uses the camera to take photos, and the microphone to record about 1.5 seconds of sound before and after each Live Photo. Styling the viewfinder and recognising faces, pets and scenes all happen on your device in real time; frames are not stored or uploaded. Photos and Live Photo videos are saved only to your photo library. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera / Microphone.

Data stored on your device

So you can continue later, the app keeps unsaved adjustment settings (not the photos themselves) and your chosen interface language in its private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and disclose it in the App Store privacy details.
