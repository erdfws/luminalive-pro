LuminaLive Pro 技术支持

遇到问题或有建议，请在 GitHub Issues 提交反馈，并尽量附上：iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、App 版本（在 App Store 页面可查看），以及问题出现时的操作步骤。

常见问题

为什么看不到我的照片？
请在“设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 照片 > LuminaLive Pro”中允许访问。如果选择了“有限访问”，只会显示你允许的照片。

编辑会覆盖原图吗？
在 App 内保存时，会在相册中生成一张新的照片或视频，原图保持不变。如果是在“照片” App 里通过编辑扩展使用本 App，修改会像系统编辑一样保存在原照片上，随时可以在“照片”中选择“复原”。

没保存就退出了，调整还在吗？
在。本 App 会在本机暂存未保存的调整，下次打开同一张照片或视频时自动恢复。

HDR、实况和人像景深会丢失吗？
本 App 会尽量保留 HDR 增益图、实况视频和人像景深数据；在不支持 HDR 的屏幕上，画面会以标准动态范围显示。

需要联网吗？
不需要。所有处理都在设备上完成，详见 隐私政策。

LuminaLive Pro Support

For problems or suggestions, open an issue on GitHub Issues. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the problem.

FAQ

Why can't I see my photos?
Allow access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > LuminaLive Pro. With limited access, only the photos you selected are shown.

Does editing overwrite the original?
Saving in the app creates a new photo or video in your library and keeps the original. When used as an editing extension inside the Photos app, changes are saved to that photo like any Photos edit and can be undone there with Revert.

I left without saving. Are my edits gone?
No. Unsaved adjustments are kept on the device and restored the next time you open the same item.

Are HDR, Live Photos and Portrait depth kept?
The app keeps HDR gain maps, Live Photo videos and Portrait depth data wherever possible; on displays without HDR, images are shown in standard dynamic range.

Does it need the internet?
No. Everything runs on your device; see the privacy policy.
